# Figure provenance

- All three SVG figures are original diagrams generated by make_figures.py using the Python standard library. No trained model was run.
- arc-and-line.svg: the diffusion arc is (sqrt(alpha_bar_t), sqrt(1 - alpha_bar_t)) for the DDPM linear schedule, beta from 0.0001 to 0.02 over 1,000 steps, with a dot every 100 steps. The flow matching line is (t, 1 - t) with a dot every 0.1. The marked arc point is step 300 (a = 0.6296, sigma = 0.7769); divided by a + sigma it lands at t = 0.4476 on the line.
- conditional-path.svg: x1 = (4.0, 2.4). Circles are 1 standard deviation of N(t x1, (1 - t)^2 I) at t = 0, 0.5, 0.9. Seven noise draws come from random.Random(3393).
- marginal-path.svg: three equally likely points (-2.0, 1.6), (2.2, 1.2), (0.4, -2.1), mean (0.20, 0.23). Shading is the closed-form mixture density p_t(x) on a 150 x 150 grid, normalized per panel and raised to the power 0.6 for visibility. Arrows are the closed-form average velocity u_t(x) = sum_i w_i (x1_i - x) / (1 - t) on a 7 x 7 grid, drawn at equal length.
